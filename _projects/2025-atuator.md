AAs a part of class, I was tasked with designing a lifting mechanism within a 2D design space measuring 150 cm in length and 50 cm in height. The challenge involved selecting a rigid bar of fixed length, three pin supports (with two required to be mounted on the ground), and a linear actuator chosen from an online catalog. For this design, I selected an actuator with a maximum force capacity of 18 kN.

The objective was to create a frame or mechanism capable of lifting the maximum possible weight to the highest achievable height, given the constraints of the design space and the rigidity of all components. The design process involved careful consideration of the placement of supports and the actuator to optimize both the lifting height and the load capacity. By analyzing the geometry and force distribution, I aimed to maximize the efficiency of the mechanism while ensuring stability and structural integrity.
